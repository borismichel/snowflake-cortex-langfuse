# Visual assets and attribution

The MIT licence covers this project's original code and documentation, not the
third-party logos, product UI or trademarks depicted in these images.

- Langfuse wordmark: [official brand assets](https://langfuse.com/brand), light SVG
  (`https://langfuse.com/brand-assets/wordmark/Langfuse/light/langfuse-wordart.svg`).
- Snowflake logo: [official brand guidelines](https://www.snowflake.com/brand-guidelines/),
  colour RGB logo from the downloadable Snowflake Logo Package.
- Product screenshots: development captures supplied by the project author on
  13 October 2026, cropped to remove account names, trace IDs and personal details.
  They show an earlier V1 run, not validation of the current routing candidate.

Logos are reproduced without recolouring or distortion to identify the systems
connected by this custom project. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement,
partnership or official support. Refer to the respective owners' brand guidance
before reusing their marks.

The composition uses the Langfuse website palette. Space Grotesk substitutes for
F37 Analog in display text; Inter is used for body text. Layout scale, rounded screenshot
cards and connector arrows are adaptations for social media. Font files are not
redistributed in this public repository.

`cortex-langfuse.png` is the README/landscape creative. `cortex-langfuse-linkedin.png`
is the portrait version. The other PNGs are unmodified screenshot crops, apart from
framing/scaling in the composed creatives. Dollar estimates within the trace viewer
are distinct from the separate `snowflake.token_credits` score.
